Legends of Music brings together two artists from very different musical worlds: Puerto Rican Latin trap artist Hanzel La H and heavy metal icon Ozzy Osbourne.

From the hard-hitting rhythms of Puerto Rico to the theatrical power of heavy metal, this site celebrates the stories and songs that make each artist memorable.
