Artist History

The backgrounds of Hanzel La H and Ozzy Osbourne show how artists from different generations and cultures can leave their own mark on music.

Puerto Rico · Latin trap

Hanzel La H

Hanzel La H is the stage name of Hainze Díaz Arroyo, a Puerto Rican rapper, singer, and producer born in Cataño, Puerto Rico. He began releasing music around 2020 and became associated with the Alqaedas Inc. circle of Latin urban artists.

His music draws from Latin trap, reggaeton, and drill. His direct delivery and street-oriented storytelling have helped him build an audience within Puerto Rico's urban music scene and among listeners of Latin trap internationally.

Tracks such as “Randy Johnson,” “Ojos Blancos,” and “Cloniao” are among the songs associated with his growing catalog. His work reflects the contemporary Puerto Rican urban scene, where regional identity, collaboration, and digital music platforms play important roles.

England · Heavy metal

Ozzy Osbourne

John Michael “Ozzy” Osbourne was born on December 3, 1948, in Aston, Birmingham, England. He rose to fame as the original lead singer of Black Sabbath, whose dark sound and influential early albums helped establish heavy metal in the late 1960s and early 1970s.

After leaving Black Sabbath, Ozzy launched a major solo career with the 1980 album Blizzard of Ozz. Songs such as “Crazy Train” and “Mr. Crowley” became defining tracks, while later releases including No More Tears introduced his music to new audiences.

Beyond recording and touring, Ozzy became known to a wider television audience through The Osbournes and helped create Ozzfest, a major touring festival for rock and metal. He reunited with Black Sabbath for later performances, including the band's farewell concert in Birmingham in July 2025. Ozzy died on July 22, 2025, leaving a lasting influence on generations of rock and metal musicians.
